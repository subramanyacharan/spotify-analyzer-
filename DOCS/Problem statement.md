# AI-Powered Review Discovery Engine — Problem Statement

## Overview

Spotify has successfully acquired millions of users and built a highly sophisticated recommendation system. However, a significant portion of listening behavior still comes from repeat playlists, familiar artists, and previously discovered tracks rather than meaningful new music discovery.

This indicates a **gap between the recommendation system's current optimization goals and users' evolving discovery needs**.

To improve music discovery, the Growth Team needs a scalable way to understand user frustrations, listening behavior, discovery challenges, and unmet needs — directly from large-scale user feedback.

---

## The Problem

Currently, user feedback is scattered across multiple channels:

- App Store reviews
- Play Store reviews
- Reddit discussions
- Community forums
- Social media conversations

This feedback is **unstructured, high-volume, noisy, and difficult to analyze manually**.

As a result, product teams lack a centralized intelligence system to continuously identify:

- Discovery-related user pain points
- Recommendation system failures
- Behavioral patterns
- Segment-specific challenges
- Emerging unmet needs

---

## Proposed Solution

Build an **AI-powered Review Discovery Engine** that automatically collects, processes, analyzes, and synthesizes user feedback at scale using **LLMs, agents, workflows, and retrieval systems**.

The system should transform raw user reviews into **actionable product insights** that help the Growth Team improve meaningful music discovery while preserving user engagement.

---

## Core Questions the AI System Must Answer

### 1. Discovery Challenges
- Why do users struggle to discover new music?
- What prevents users from exploring beyond familiar content?

### 2. Recommendation Frustrations
- What are the most common frustrations with Spotify's recommendation system?
- Are recommendations perceived as repetitive, irrelevant, biased, or low quality?

### 3. Listening Intent & Behavior
- What listening behaviors are users trying to achieve?
- Are users seeking discovery, comfort listening, mood-based listening, passive listening, or contextual listening?

### 4. Repeat Listening Behavior
- What causes users to repeatedly listen to the same playlists, artists, or songs?
- Is repeat listening driven by preference, habit, algorithmic bias, or lack of discovery quality?

### 5. User Segmentation
- Which user segments face different discovery challenges?
- Examples:
  - New users
  - Casual listeners
  - Premium users
  - Heavy listeners
  - Niche music listeners

### 6. Unmet Needs
- What unmet discovery needs consistently emerge across user feedback?
- What opportunities exist for improving music exploration?

---

## Expected Output

The AI Review Discovery Engine should provide structured insights such as:

- Top discovery-related complaints
- Major recommendation pain points
- Key listening behavior patterns
- Segment-wise discovery challenges
- Emerging unmet user needs
- Trend shifts over time

---

## Success Criteria

The system is successful if it can:

- ✅ Process large-scale review data automatically
- ✅ Classify major themes accurately
- ✅ Identify recurring discovery-related pain points
- ✅ Segment users by behavior and challenges
- ✅ Surface actionable product opportunities for the Growth Team

---

## One-Line Summary

> Build an AI-powered review intelligence system that analyzes large-scale user feedback to identify why users struggle with music discovery, why repetitive listening persists, and what product opportunities exist to improve meaningful discovery in Spotify.
